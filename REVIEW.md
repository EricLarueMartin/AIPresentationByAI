# Reviewing the guide

Open a topic in your chosen audience and language. Select **Review this slide** to save a decision and a note in the browser. **Export all review notes** downloads the collected feedback. A review decision records a proposal. The published source changes when an editor updates the repository.

The full guide numbers topics 1 through 37. Shorter talks retain those numbers so participants can find the same topic later. The counter also shows its position within the selected route. Current links use fragments such as `#slide-6`. Older numeric and lettered links still open the original topic when it is available. Reviews remain attached to their topic when slide numbers change.

Getting started uses its own numbered lessons. Links such as `?audience=starter&route=all&view=read#start-chat` open the tutorial directly. Select a duration to rehearse a shorter sequence. Tutorial review notes belong to the lesson where you write them.

Check a change in Read, Present and Discuss. Read should work on its own. Present can use a figure, a line of prose, a comparison, a procedure or a list. Let the subject determine the structure. Keep dates, evaluation conditions and primary references with empirical claims. The decay fit uses synthetic observations.

Spanish is an AI-assisted translation awaiting independent language review. Timings are rehearsal budgets. The 30- and 60-minute routes reserve five and ten minutes for discussion respectively.
