# Reviewing the guide

Open a topic in your chosen audience and language. Select **Review this slide** to save a decision and a note in the browser. **Export all review notes** downloads the collected feedback. A review decision records a proposal. The published source changes when an editor updates the repository.

The full guide numbers topics 1 through 38. Shorter talks retain those numbers so participants can find the same topic later. The counter also shows its position within the selected route. Earlier lettered links redirect to the corresponding numbered topic. A numeric link always opens the current slide with that number.

Check a change in Read, Present and Discuss. Read should work on its own. Present can use a figure, a line of prose, a comparison, a procedure or a list. Let the subject determine the structure. Keep dates, evaluation conditions and primary references with empirical claims. The decay fit uses synthetic observations. The letter-count exercise invites a live comparison.

Spanish is an AI-assisted translation awaiting independent language review. Timings are rehearsal budgets. The 30- and 60-minute routes reserve five and ten minutes for discussion respectively.
